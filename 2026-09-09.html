✏️ Daryl’s Key Corrections

Use the past participle after “have”
Your wording“I didn’t heard of that.”
Daryl’s correction“I haven’t heard of it.”
Why? After have or haven’t, use the past participle heard. This form means the information is new to you up to the present moment.
Say “in the morning”
Your wording“When I get up morning, I check the weather.”
Daryl’s correction“When I get up in the morning, I check the weather.”
Why? Use the fixed phrase in the morning. You can also say, “When I wake up, I check the day’s weather.”
Use passive voice for receiving an effect
Your wording“Maybe we could affect.”
Daryl’s correction“Maybe we could be affected.”
Why? Korea receives the effect, so use be affected. The active form needs an object: “The smoke could affect Korea.”
Describe canceled school activities clearly
Your wording“The school was prohibited. Students go out outside physical education.”
Daryl’s guided version“They don’t have PE on those days.”
Natural expanded version“On high-pollution days, schools cancel outdoor PE classes.”
Why? A school is not normally “prohibited.” State exactly which activity is canceled or suspended.

💬 Useful Language

weather station

A place equipped to measure local weather conditions.

projected trajectory

The expected path of a storm or other moving system.

fine-dust advisory

An official warning about unhealthy levels of airborne particles.

be affected by

Receive an influence or consequence: “Korea can be affected by pollution from other regions.”

night owl

Someone who naturally stays awake and functions well late at night.

body clock

Your natural daily rhythm for sleeping, waking, and feeling alert.

track my sleep

Record and review sleep duration or quality with a device.

designed for

Made with a particular purpose in mind: “This watch is designed for outdoor activities.”
