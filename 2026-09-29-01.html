🚀 Priority Patterns & Suggestions

1. Use complete preposition chunks

Several ideas were clear but missed the preposition that English requires, including “stay a hotel” and “told to my wife.”

Try: Memorize the noun or verb together with its partner: stay at a hotel, speak to someone, tell someone something.

  • “We usually stay at an affordable hotel.”
  • “I told my wife that we would not use all the amenities.”

2. Check third-person verb endings

When describing your wife's habits, verbs sometimes lost the -s ending: “my wife book” and “she always write.”

Try: After he, she, or it, quickly check the main verb before continuing.

  • “My wife books the hotel.”
  • “She always writes that it is a honeymoon trip.”

3. Separate countable and uncountable nouns

Plural forms such as “beautiful sceneries” sounded unnatural because scenery is normally uncountable. Similar noun choices affected clarity elsewhere.

Try: Learn common travel nouns in full chunks: beautiful scenery, autumn foliage, hotel amenities.

  • “My mother wants to see the beautiful autumn scenery.”
  • “We do not have enough time to use the hotel amenities.”

4. Restart with one complete clause

You often noticed an error and repaired it, which is useful, but several restarts accumulated inside the same sentence.

Try: Pause and begin again with “What I mean is …” followed by one subject and one main verb.

  • “What I mean is that our relationship has changed.”
  • “Let me start again. I think we are ready to have a child now.”

5. Organize comparisons with a fixed frame

Your hotel preferences included good reasons, but the comparison became difficult to follow when several ideas appeared in one sentence.

Try: Use preference + because + exception to make the logic easy to follow.

  • “I prefer affordable hotels because we spend most of the day outside.”
  • “A luxury hotel can be worth it, especially for a honeymoon.”

✏️ Key Corrections

Tap a correction to see the revision and explanation.

1. Spending a holiday with family
Your wording“Last Thursday and Friday, I enjoyed holidays in my family.”
Minimal correction“Last Thursday and Friday, I enjoyed the holidays with my family.”
Natural version“I spent last Thursday and Friday celebrating Chuseok with my family.”
Why? Use with, not in, for the people accompanying you. Spend a holiday with family is more natural than enjoy a holiday in my family.
2. Going outside the city
Your wording“I think I can go to suburban or rural areas.”
Minimal correction“I think I can go to a suburban or rural area.”
Natural version“We might take a day trip to the countryside.”
Why? Singular countable area needs an article. Take a day trip to the countryside is a natural phrase for this kind of outing.
3. Describing autumn views
Your wording“She wants to see beautiful sceneries, fall sceneries.”
Minimal correction“She wants to see beautiful fall scenery.”
Natural version“She wants to see the beautiful autumn foliage.”
Why? Scenery is normally uncountable, so it does not take a plural -s. Autumn foliage specifically means colorful fall leaves.
4. Reporting what you said
Your wording“I told to my wife that …”
Minimal correction“I told my wife that …”
Natural version“I asked my wife why we should pay for an expensive hotel just to rest.”
Why? Tell takes a person directly: tell someone. The natural version uses ask someone why to report your original question smoothly.
5. Staying at a hotel
Your wording“I don't have to stay a very expensive hotel.”
Minimal correction“I don't have to stay at a very expensive hotel.”
Natural version“I don't need to stay at a luxury hotel.”
Why? The verb stay needs at before a hotel. Don't need to and luxury hotel sound more natural in this context.
6. Describing your wife's habit
Your wording“Whenever my wife book a hotel, she always write …”
Minimal correction“Whenever my wife books a hotel, she always writes …”
Natural version“Whenever my wife books a hotel, she says we're celebrating our honeymoon.”
Why? In the present simple, verbs after she need -s: books and writes. The natural version also avoids repeating always after whenever.
7. Life before children
Your wording“You should enjoy your life before the kid.”
Minimal correction“You should enjoy your life before having a child.”
Natural version“You should make the most of life before you have children.”
Why? Before the kid does not clearly express the event of becoming a parent. Make the most of means to enjoy an opportunity fully.
8. Explaining how your relationship changed
Your wording“We were changed. We have been changed.”
Minimal correction“We have changed.”
Natural version“Our relationship has changed, and I think we're ready now.”
Why? Use active have changed when the change happened naturally. Have been changed suggests that an outside person or force changed you.

My Hotel Preferences

For a regular trip, I usually prefer an affordable hotel because my wife and I spend most of the day exploring. We often return at eight, nine, or even ten at night, so we do not have enough time to use all the amenities. In that situation, an expensive room does not feel worth it to me. However, I understand why some people enjoy a hotel vacation. A comfortable bed, good service, and a nice pool can make the hotel part of the experience. I would consider a luxury hotel for a special occasion, especially a honeymoon, but not for every trip.
