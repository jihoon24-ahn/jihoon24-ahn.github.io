Explaining an idiom: “Then it hit me”

"The expression then it hit me can have two meanings. It can mean that something physically struck someone, or that the person suddenly understood something. In the frisbee joke, both meanings work at the same time. The speaker notices that the frisbee is getting bigger and then realizes why: it is coming closer. At the same moment, it actually hits the speaker. That is what makes it a pun. I understood the figurative meaning as getting the solution. To explain the joke clearly, I would describe the situation first and then explain the two meanings of the same phrase."
