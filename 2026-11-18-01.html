✏️ Key Corrections

Tap a correction to see the revision and explanation.

1. Saying where your winter clothes are
Your wording"Heavy clothing is my... heavy clothing are in my parents' house."
Minimal correction"My heavy clothing is in my parents' house."
Natural version"My heavy winter clothes are at my parents' place."
Why? Clothing is uncountable, so it takes is. Clothes is plural and takes are. People keep things at someone's place.
2. Describing the seasonal switch
Your wording"A few weeks ago, I brought some clothing to my home... ah to my studio apartment. And I brought some summer clothing to my parents' house."
Minimal correction"A few weeks ago, I brought some clothing to my studio apartment. And I took some summer clothing to my parents' house."
Natural version"A few weeks ago, I brought my winter clothes here and took my summer clothes to my parents' place."
Why? Use bring for movement toward where you are now and take for movement away. Your studio is here, so bring; your parents' house is there, so take.
3. Talking about switching to hot coffee
Your wording"So maybe if it's more colder than now, I will switch my coffee from iced to hot."
Minimal correction"So maybe if it's colder than now, I will switch my coffee from iced to hot."
Natural version"If it gets any colder, I'll probably switch from iced to hot coffee."
Why? Colder already means more cold, so more colder doubles it. If it gets colder describes a change in the weather naturally.
4. Explaining your first winter here
Your wording"This is my first winter since I moved here in studio apartment, so I don't know yet."
Minimal correction"This is my first winter since I moved into this studio apartment, so I don't know yet."
Natural version"This is my first winter in this studio, so I'll have to see."
Why? You move into a home, not move in it. The studio is a specific one, so it needs this.
5. Talking about your budget
Your wording"Sometimes I wish it would be a little bit bigger than now, but in my budget it is... ah it is good. In my budget, it is the best thing I could do."
Minimal correction"Sometimes I wish it were a little bit bigger, but within my budget, it is the best thing I could find."
Natural version"I sometimes wish it were a bit bigger, but it's the best I can afford."
Why? After I wish, use the past form: I wish it were. You live within a budget, not in it. The best I can afford says it in five words.
6. Telling the story of your second home
Your wording"So I lived two years and... during the... when I stayed, I met my wife. So I bought a house, my another apartment, and I moved there with my wife."
Minimal correction"So I lived there for two years, and while I stayed there, I met my wife. So I bought another apartment and moved there with my wife."
Natural version"I lived there for two years, and during that time I met my wife. Then we bought a two-bedroom apartment and moved in together."
Why? A length of time needs for: lived there for two years. My and another can't be used together; choose another apartment.
7. Explaining your wife's job
Your wording"Now my wife is abroad to work, so I don't need a big house."
Minimal correction"Now my wife is abroad for work, so I don't need a big house."
Natural version"My wife is working abroad right now, so I don't need a big place."
Why? Abroad to work sounds like a purpose clause. Say working abroad or abroad for work.
8. Describing your future plan
Your wording"And when she will come back to South Korea, then we will move to that apartment in Seoul."
Minimal correction"And when she comes back to South Korea, we will move to that apartment in Seoul."
Natural version"When she comes back to Korea, we'll move into our apartment in Seoul."
Why? After when, if, after, and before, use the present for a future event: when she comes back. Keep will in the main clause, and drop then.

switch out (clothes)

Meaning
To replace one season's clothes with another's.

Example
"I switch out my summer and winter clothes every November."

bear the cold

Meaning
To tolerate cold weather.

Example
"I can still bear the cold, so I'm drinking iced coffee."

Meaning
To live alone, without family or roommates.

Example
"I loved living on my own after I started working."

Example

common practice

Meaning

Meaning
