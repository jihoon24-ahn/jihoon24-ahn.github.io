2. Complete articles and determiners

  • “I thought the United States was an interesting country.”
  • “I don’t have any special plans for the holiday.”

4. Match tense and comparison to the timeline

5. Restart long ideas with one clean sentence

✏️ Key Corrections

Tap a correction to see the revision and explanation.

1. Explain why you missed the call
Your wording“I’m sorry, I couldn’t get, I couldn’t response the phone.”
Minimal correction“I’m sorry, I couldn’t answer the phone.”
Natural version“Sorry I missed your call; I was taking out the trash.”
Why? Response is a noun; the verb is respond, normally followed by to. For a phone call, answer the phone or miss a call is more natural.
2. Compare today with your normal routine
Your wording“Actually today I have an important meeting so I woke up earlier than before.”
Minimal correction“Actually, today I have an important meeting, so I woke up earlier than usual.”
Natural version“I have an important meeting today, so I woke up earlier than usual.”
Why? Use earlier than usual when comparing today with your normal routine. Earlier than before usually compares two specific previous times.
3. Describe your holiday plans
Your wording“I don’t have special plans.”
Minimal correction“I don’t have any special plans.”
Natural version“I don’t have any special plans for the holiday.”
Why? In negative sentences, any naturally emphasizes that there are no plans. Adding for the holiday makes the reference complete.
4. Explain why English is powerful
Your wording“English is a powerful language to communicate a lot of people from many countries.”
Minimal correction“English is a powerful language for communicating with a lot of people from many countries.”
Natural version“English is a powerful tool for communicating with people from many different countries.”
Why? The verb pattern is communicate with someone. A powerful tool for also sounds natural when describing how a language helps you achieve a goal.
5. Describe your impression of the United States
Your wording“I thought United States are interesting country.”
Minimal correction“I thought the United States was an interesting country.”
Natural version“I found the United States to be a fascinating country.”
Why? The country name requires the, and it takes the singular verb was. The singular countable noun country also needs an.
6. Pause to organize your thoughts
Your wording“Give a second. Let me tell you again. I wanted to organize this information.”
Minimal correction“Give me a second. Let me tell you again. I want to organize this information.”
Natural version“Give me a second to organize my thoughts. Let me try that again.”
Why? Give needs the indirect object me. Because you are organizing the idea now, use want, not wanted. Organize my thoughts is the natural fixed phrase.
7. Ask why Mary moved to Mexico
Your wording“So what brings you in Mexico?”
Minimal correction“So what brought you to Mexico?”
Natural version“What originally brought you to Mexico?”
Why? Her move happened in the past, so use brought. A destination takes to, not in.
8. Ask about speaking Spanish
Your wording“Okay, so can I ask about Spanish? Do you Spanish?”
Minimal correction“Okay, can I ask about Spanish? Do you speak Spanish?”
Natural version“Can I ask—do you speak Spanish?”
Why? A language name is a noun here, so the question needs the verb speak. The natural version combines the introduction and question smoothly.
